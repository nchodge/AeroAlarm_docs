# AeroAlarm Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**App:** AeroAlarm  
**Bundle ID:** com.aeroalarm.app

## Overview

AeroAlarm is a UTC (Zulu time) alarm clock. We designed it to work without accounts, analytics, or cloud sync. Your alarm data stays on your device.

## Data We Collect

**AeroAlarm does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.**

We do not operate servers for this app and do not receive the alarm times, settings, or usage information you enter.

## Data Stored on Your Device

AeroAlarm stores the following locally on your phone or tablet so the app can function:

| Data | Purpose | Location |
|------|---------|----------|
| Alarm times and on/off state | Schedule and display your alarms | On-device storage (AsyncStorage / app preferences) |
| Snooze state | Resume countdown after app restart | On-device storage |
| Haptic-before-sound preference | Apply your alarm sound setting | On-device storage |

This data is not uploaded to us or any third party by the app.

## Permissions

AeroAlarm requests platform permissions only so alarms can ring reliably:

- **Alarms / exact alarms:** Schedule wake-up alarms at the UTC time you choose
- **Notifications (platform-dependent):** Deliver alarm alerts
- **Full-screen intent (Android):** Show the alarm on a locked screen
- **Vibration:** Optional haptic alert before or with sound

Granting these permissions does not send your data to AeroAlarm developers.

## Third Parties

AeroAlarm does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social login.

Alarm scheduling uses native iOS and Android alarm APIs through the app’s built-in components. Those operating systems may process alarm schedules according to their own privacy policies.

## Children’s Privacy

AeroAlarm does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app is suitable for general audiences and does not require an account.

## Data Retention and Deletion

All app data remains on your device until you:

- Delete individual alarms in the app
- Turn alarms off
- Uninstall AeroAlarm

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored alarm data from your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when that happens. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
